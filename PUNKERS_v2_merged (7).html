Getting started is simple — stake your Punker NFT and you'll start earning $PUNK immediately. But keep in mind, once you're in, the clock starts ticking.

  • Stake: Lock your Punker NFT into the staking contract to start earning
  • Earn Daily: Your NFT earns $PUNK every day automatically based on its rarity
  • Pay Tax: You must pay daily tax from your earnings to keep your NFT alive
  • No Unstake: Once you stake, there is no unstake button. You're in the game until you survive or die
  • Transfer or List = Auto Unstake: If you send your NFT to another wallet or list it on any marketplace, it automatically gets unstaked and all unclaimed $PUNK is forfeited

💀 DEATH & GRAVEYARD

Every Punker falls into one of two categories — alive or dead. There's no in-between.

Survivors:

  • Active NFTs that are staked and paying tax
  • Continue earning $PUNK daily
  • Visible on the leaderboard

Graveyard:

  • NFTs that were executed by other players for unpaid tax
  • Dead NFTs stay in your wallet but can never earn $PUNK again
  • Permanent death — no revival, no second chance, no exceptions
  • Displayed in the Graveyard as a memorial for fallen Punkers
  • Dead NFTs can be sold or transferred, but death follows the token — the new owner cannot stake or earn with it either
Once your Punker is executed, it's gone from the game forever. There is no way to bring it back — not even by selling it to someone else. Death is tied to the token ID, not the wallet. Stay alive.
🌸 DEAD BUT NOT FORGOTTEN

When your Punker dies, you can't stake, earn, or do anything with it in-game anymore. But other players can visit your fallen Punker in the Graveyard and leave a flower as a tribute. That's it. Death is permanent.
